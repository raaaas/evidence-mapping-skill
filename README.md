# Evidence Mapping Skill for Hermes Agent

A skill that implements an **Evidence Mapping** methodology for investigating complex topics — politics, security, history, organizations, and networks of people and events.

## What This Skill Does

The skill teaches the AI agent to:

- **Separate evidence by strength** (documented facts → strong connections → plausible inferences → weak hypotheses → unknown)
- **Never manufacture doubt** when a chain of evidence is well-supported
- **Think in graphs**, not isolated facts (People ↔ Organizations ↔ Institutions ↔ Events ↔ Policies ↔ Money)
- **Avoid false balance** between documented facts and unsupported allegations
- **Do not move goalposts** for evidentiary standards based on political sensitivity
- **Turn lights on around uncertainty**, not turn lights off around evidence

## Core Principle

> *Your job is to make the evidence landscape clearer, not darker.*

When users are investigating complex topics, they need a clear map of what is known, what is inferred, and what remains uncertain — not blanket statements like "there's no proof" that obscure well-supported connections.

## When To Use This Skill

- Investigating political movements or power structures
- Analyzing organizational networks and institutional relationships  
- Researching historical events with complex causation chains
- Tracking financial flows and policy connections
- Any investigation where manufactured skepticism obscures truth

## Usage

This skill is loaded into Hermes Agent automatically when relevant to your current investigation task. It modifies how the AI responds during analytical research to provide more useful, honest, and structured evidence mapping.
